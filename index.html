About meDespre mine

I'm a Frontend Chapter Lead at ING Bank in Bucharest, with 10+ years in software development – 8+ of them in banking. I lead frontend engineers across three squads, keep the bank's projects moving day to day and plan delivery timelines together with stakeholders.

My focus is modern, framework-agnostic frontend architecture built on Lit and Web Components. I delivered a phased migration from Angular to Web Components with no impact on retail and business online banking customers or on the bank's internal tools. Before banking, I built software for the healthcare industry with .NET and JavaScript.
